FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Question not listed? Email info@threadix.nl.

What does an engagement cost?

Depending on the work, we use a fixed price or a day rate. For ongoing guidance or management we agree a fixed monthly fee. You always get a quote first.

What areas can you help with?

Consulting and advice, purchasing and sales, supply chain and logistics, IT and data, and project management and support. These often overlap, and that is exactly the strength.

Do you only advise, or also deliver?

Both. We don't stop at a report: we guide the delivery and stay involved through management where wanted.

Do you work for smaller businesses too?

Yes. We tailor the approach to the size and the question, from a one-off advice to long-term guidance.

How quickly can you start?

We decide that together. After an introduction we make a proposal with a realistic timeline.

Can it be extended later?

Yes. Extra work, hours or connections are offered with a quote up front.
