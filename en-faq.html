FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Question not listed? Email info@threadix.nl.

What does a solution cost?

A fixed price for implementation, then a fixed monthly fee for licence, maintenance and support. You always get a quote first.

Which systems can you connect to?

Most ERP systems and databases, such as SQL Server, Navision/Business Central and Sage X3. Using something else? We will look at the options together.

What happens to our data?

Your data stays yours and stays in your own systems. We only read what the solution needs and store nothing on our side.

How quickly does a solution go live?

A simple dashboard is often live within a few weeks, once access to the data is arranged.

What if something breaks?

Report it to info@threadix.nl. We respond within one working day and fix it as quickly as possible, also when the cause is outside our control, such as an internet outage.

Can it be extended later?

Yes. Extra screens, features or connections are offered with a quote up front.
