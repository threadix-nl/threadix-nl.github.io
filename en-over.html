About Threadix

Threads that come together

Threadix was founded in Tilburg with one goal: helping businesses move forward by connecting advice and delivery.

The story

Why Threadix

Tilburg was once the textile city of the Netherlands, where loose threads were spun and woven into strong fabric. Threadix works the same way with businesses: connecting separate efforts, people and systems into one whole that works.

The name comes from thread. The three lines in the logo stand for advice, delivery and management: the things we bring together.

Project managementGuiding projects from start to go-live, and into management afterwards.
